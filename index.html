Our department

Curiosity meets practical learning

The Department of Computer Science introduces students to the principles behind modern computing. Classes combine lectures, laboratory work and team projects so learners can apply concepts to real problems.

Students build skills in programming, data, systems and responsible technology while learning to communicate and collaborate.
